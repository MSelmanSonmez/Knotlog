# Facts

One line, one fact: a bullet line carries exactly one fact; a second one
earns its own line. Every bullet opens with one of three tags.

Preference — how you like to work: tools, formats, tone.
Decision — a made choice, valid until changed: the choice and its reason.
Convention — how things run in this vault: names, layout, flow.

A line belongs here only if it will still be both true and useful months
from now: identity qualifies, news does not. What goes stale is news — it
belongs in Threads.md or the session notes, not here.

## Facts

- Decision: <a project choice> stays <state>; <the reason>.
- Convention: an English vault names its files in English.
